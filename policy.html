Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

JM WebDev (“I”, “me”, “my”) operates the website https://jonmckenziewebdev.co.uk. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information is collected through this website and how it is used.

Contact Details

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, you can contact me at:

Email: jonmckenziewebdev@gmail.com

Information I collect

When you use the contact form on this website, I collect the information you provide:

  • Name
  • Email address

I do not collect any additional personal information through the contact form.

How your information is used

I use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Communicate with you about your message
  • Provide information you have requested

I do not use your information for marketing purposes and I do not send newsletters.

How contact form messages are processed

This website uses EmailJS to send contact form submissions to my email address (jonmckenziewebdev@gmail.com). When you submit the form, your name and email address are processed by EmailJS in order to deliver your message.

You can read EmailJS’s privacy information here

Legal basis for processing

Under UK data protection law, the legal basis for processing your contact form information is legitimate interests — specifically, responding to enquiries sent through the website.

Data retention

I keep contact form emails only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry and maintain relevant correspondence. Unneeded emails are deleted periodically.

Cookies

I do not intentionally use cookies for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes.

Some third-party services used by the website, such as content delivery networks (CDNs), may technically set or access cookies as part of delivering website resources. I do not use these cookies to identify visitors or track their behaviour.

Third-party services

This website loads icons from the Font Awesome CDN. When your browser requests these icon files, Font Awesome may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the files.

You can read Font Awesome’s privacy policy here

Your rights

If you are in the UK, you have rights under UK data protection law, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)

ICO Website: https://www.ico.org.uk/

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information you submit through the website. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
